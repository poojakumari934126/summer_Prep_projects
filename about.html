Our Founding Journey

Starting as a small food-tech experiment in 2024, FoodieHub expanded to partner with over 200 executive chefs, cloud kitchens, and premium restaurants. We reimagined food delivery by putting food temperature, ingredient quality, and customer satisfaction first.

Today, our algorithm matches your food order with the nearest master chef and ultra-fast rider to guarantee steaming hot deliveries within 30 minutes.
